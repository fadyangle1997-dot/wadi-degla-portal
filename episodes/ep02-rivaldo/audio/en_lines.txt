On the very day he was named / the best player in Europe…
his relationship with Barça's coach / fell apart.
And the best player in the world… / found himself on the bench.
But believe me… / that wasn't the hardest thing he faced.
Because before Barcelona… / before the Ballon d'Or…
this boy grew up / poorer than you can imagine.
Malnutrition left his body weak, / and he lost several teeth as a child.
He walked long distances / just to get to training…
because, simply put, / he couldn't afford the bus fare.
That boy's name was… / Rivaldo Vítor Borba Ferreira.
Born in 1972 in Recife,
in north-east Brazil, / into a very poor family.
But one person believed / his son could become a great player…
his father, Romildo.
He encouraged him, / followed him,
and dreamed with him.
But in 1989… / tragedy struck.
Rivaldo's father died in an accident.
And Rivaldo decided to quit football.
For him, the dream was over.
But his mother refused.
She said: keep going…
because it was the dream / his father believed in.
And Rivaldo kept going.
He started at Santa Cruz,
then Mogi Mirim, / then Corinthians,
and Palmeiras… / until Europe started to notice him.
1996…
Rivaldo arrived in Spain / through Deportivo La Coruña.
And one season was all it took…
for Barcelona / to decide they wanted him.
Summer 1997…
Barcelona had just lost / Ronaldo Nazário,
who had moved to Inter Milan.
The club needed / a new Brazilian star.
And the choice fell on Rivaldo.
And that's where… / the key chapter of his story began.
In his first two seasons at Barcelona… / Rivaldo won La Liga twice,
and the Copa del Rey once.
But what happened in 1999… / officially put him on top of the world.
21 December 1999.
The Ballon d'Or winner is announced.
David Beckham was coming off / a historic season,
having won the treble / with Manchester United.
But the result was…
Beckham: 154 points.
Rivaldo: 219.
Rivaldo wins the Ballon d'Or.
And he also wins FIFA's / World Player of the Year for 1999.
The boy who struggled / just to get to training…
became the best player in the world.
It should have been / his happiest time…
Right?
No.
Because with the Ballon d'Or…
came one of the strangest crises / of Rivaldo's time at Barcelona.
Louis van Gaal wanted him / on the left wing.
Rivaldo felt his best position / was through the middle…
behind the strikers.
The dispute grew…
and Rivaldo said he no longer wanted / to play on the wing.
Van Gaal answered in his own way.
He dropped the best player in the world / from the team.
The Ballon d'Or in his hands… / and his place in the side not guaranteed.
But if you want to know / what kind of player Rivaldo was…
you only need to watch one night.
17 June 2001.
The final matchday of La Liga.
Barcelona v Valencia… / at the Camp Nou.
The maths was simple: / Barcelona had to win…
to qualify for the Champions League.
And Rivaldo produced one / of his greatest games for Barcelona.
The first goal…
a direct free kick.
1-0.
Valencia equalise.
Rivaldo gets the ball far out…
and shoots.
A second goal.
2-1.
Valencia equalise again.
2-2.
And time is running out.
With that score…
Barcelona are out of the Champions League.
The 87th minute.
Frank de Boer sends the ball / towards the box.
Rivaldo takes it on his chest…
the ball still in the air…
he throws himself backwards…
a bicycle kick.
Goal.
3-2.
A hat-trick for Rivaldo.
Barcelona are in the Champions League.
The Camp Nou erupted.
And in a single moment… / Rivaldo saved Barcelona's season…
with one of the most famous goals / in the club's history.
But his story still / needed one final scene.
Summer 2002.
Rivaldo lines up with Brazil / in the World Cup final.
Brazil against Germany.
Brazil win 2-0… / and Rivaldo becomes a world champion.
But back from the World Cup…
he found Van Gaal back / in charge of Barcelona.
And Rivaldo decided to leave.
From a child who knew poverty and hunger…
who lost his father / while just starting out…
to a player who held the Ballon d'Or…
who scored an impossible goal / to save Barcelona on one of its biggest nights…
and then lifted the World Cup / wearing Brazil's No. 10.
That was the story of…
Rivaldo.
One of the greatest Brazilians / ever to wear the Barcelona shirt.
In the next episode…
we'll talk about another Brazilian / who joined Barça a year after Rivaldo left.
But this time… / the smile was part of the game.
Ronaldinho.
